Shipping, Care & Payments

Everything between "I want this" and it being draped at your event.

Shipping & Delivery

  • Dispatched from Chennai within 2–3 working days
  • Ships across India — packed flat, with soft tissue and a branded pouch
  • Trackable courier; WhatsApp updates once handed over
  • Payments: UPI, bank transfer or cash on delivery (on select cities)

Looking After Your Saree

  • Dry clean first; hand wash in cold water with mild detergent later
  • Iron on the reverse, or steam while slightly damp
  • Fold with the pallu inner-most; store away from direct light
  • Blouse piece: 0.80 m running, in a deliberate contrast shade
